## 5. Response handling rules

- **Positive interest:** Thank the person and ask about credentials, relevant expertise, availability, proposed fee, scope, timeline, and any institutional requirements. Do not assume they have accepted the assignment.
- **Request for more information:** Send only the requested project summary / review scope. Do not send unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
- **Decline:** Thank them, mark the contact as declined, and do not send reminders.
- **Referral:** Record the referral as a lead, then verify the person's institutional role and suitability before contacting them.
- **Fee quote:** Record it as a quote only. No paid engagement or commitment without explicit owner approval.
- **No response:** Follow the schedule above, then pause contact.
- **Medical review:** Only a qualified and appropriately scoped professional's explicit written sign-off counts as approval. An acknowledgement, informal comment, or sexology/psychology review alone does not constitute medical approval for all health claims.
