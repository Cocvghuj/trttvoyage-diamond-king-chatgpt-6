## Blogger upload procedure

For each article, work in **Blogger → Posts → New post**:

1. Copy the post title from this sheet into Blogger's title field.
2. Open the corresponding HTML source file and copy only the article markup inside `<article class="trtt-wellness-article"...>` through its closing `</article>`. Do not include the top SEO/status HTML comments.
3. Switch Blogger's editor to HTML view and paste the markup.
4. In Post settings, enter the exact search description and custom permalink slug from this sheet. Blogger may expose custom permalink under the permalink setting; confirm the generated URL before saving.
5. Add the labels exactly as listed, separated into individual labels.
6. Add a genuine, licensed featured image. Record its source/licence and meaningful alt text. Do not publish with an empty image placeholder or a guessed URL.
7. Save as **Draft** first. Preview mobile and desktop, inspect links and formatting, then request editorial and qualified medical review where relevant.
8. Only publish after all review checks pass and the site owner explicitly approves live publication.
