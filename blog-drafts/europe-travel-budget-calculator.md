## Plan your European trip budget in one place

Planning a trip across Europe is exciting, but costs can quickly become difficult to track. Accommodation, meals, local transport, sightseeing and long-distance travel all add up. The free **TRTT Voyage Europe Travel Budget Calculator** helps you organise those estimates in one simple tool.

Enter the number of travellers and trip days, choose EUR, USD or GBP, and add your expected costs. The calculator shows an estimated group total, a category-by-category breakdown and the estimated cost per traveller.

## What can you calculate?

- Flights and long-distance transport for the whole group
- Accommodation per room or rental per night
- Food per person per day
- Local transport per person per day
- Activities and sightseeing for the group
- Other trip expenses

You can also print the summary or use your browser’s print dialog to save it as a PDF.

## How to use the calculator

1. Enter your destination, number of travellers and trip length.
2. Select the currency in which you want to view your estimate.
3. Add your expected costs in each category.
4. Select **Calculate budget** to see the group total and per-traveller estimate.
5. Adjust your figures as your plans change.

## Important planning note

The calculator is an estimate tool. It does not search live fares, check hotel availability or convert currencies using exchange rates. Enter all amounts in the currency you selected. Accommodation is calculated as one room or rental total per night; food and local transport are calculated per person per day.
