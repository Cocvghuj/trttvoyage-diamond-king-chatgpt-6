## Plan your European trip budget in one place

The TRTT Voyage Europe Travel Budget Calculator helps travellers organise their own estimates for accommodation, meals, local transport, sightseeing and long-distance travel. Enter the number of travellers and trip days, choose EUR, USD or GBP, and enter your expected costs to see a group total, category breakdown and per-traveller estimate.

## What can you estimate?

- Flights and long-distance transport for the whole group
- Accommodation per room or rental per night
- Food per person per day
- Local transport per person per day
- Activities and sightseeing for the group
- Other trip expenses

You can print the summary or use your browser’s print dialog to save it as a PDF.

## How to use the calculator

1. Enter your destination, number of travellers and trip length.
2. Select the currency used for all amounts you enter.
3. Add expected costs in each category.
4. Select **Calculate budget** to view the group total and per-traveller estimate.
5. Adjust your figures as plans change.

## Important planning note

This is an estimate tool, not a live quote service. It does not search fares, check hotel availability or convert currencies using exchange rates. Enter every amount in the selected currency. Accommodation is calculated as one room or rental total per night; food and local transport are calculated per person per day.
