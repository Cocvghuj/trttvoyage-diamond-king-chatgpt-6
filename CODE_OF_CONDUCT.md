# Code of Conduct

TRTT Voyage project spaces should remain respectful, useful, accessible, and evidence-focused.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and specific examples.
- Welcome beginner questions and different backgrounds.
- Critique work and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy; never publish credentials, private messages, or personal information without consent.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest and affiliations.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, deliberate disruption, spam, impersonation, malicious code, and disclosure of private or secret information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting
Maintainers may moderate contributions or restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the Owner through an established private channel. Do not expose sensitive evidence in a public issue.
