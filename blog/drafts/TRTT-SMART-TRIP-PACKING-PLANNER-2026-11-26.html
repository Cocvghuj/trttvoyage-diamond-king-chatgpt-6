Preparing for a trip is exciting, but remembering every essential can be harder than choosing the destination. A simple checklist can reduce last-minute stress and help you focus on the journey.

1. Start with the kind of trip

A beach break, city weekend, outdoor adventure and business trip all require different items. The TRTT Voyage Smart Trip Packing Planner adapts its suggested checklist to the trip type and weather you select.

2. Build a practical essentials list

The checklist starts with travel documents, payment methods, devices, clothing, toiletries and useful extras. Treat it as a starting point—not a substitute for checking your own needs.

3. Track what is packed

Tick items as you pack and watch the progress indicator update. This makes it easier to spot what remains before you leave.

4. Export or print your list

When your list is ready, export a plain-text copy or print it. Keep important travel documents securely stored and accessible offline.

5. Check the details before departure

Packing needs depend on destination, season, airline baggage rules, personal requirements and planned activities. Confirm entry requirements and any medication or health guidance through official sources before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the packing planner free?

Yes. The browser-based tool is designed to be used without a subscription or account.

Does it upload my trip details?

The tool is designed to work locally in your browser. It does not send trip details to a TRTT Voyage server. Your browser and any extensions still operate under their own privacy settings.

Does the checklist guarantee I have everything?

No. It is a practical reminder, not a guarantee. Add personal items and verify official travel requirements for your journey.
